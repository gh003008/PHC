### 2. Reward 함수 구조
현재 `_compute_reward()` (humanoid_im_vic.py:1048):

**Imitation Reward** (가중합):
- `r_body_pos` (w=0.4): exp(-200 * position_error²)
- `r_body_rot` (w=0.3): exp(-10 * rotation_error²)
- `r_vel` (w=0.2): exp(-1.0 * velocity_error²)
- `r_ang_vel` (w=0.1): exp(-0.1 * angular_velocity_error²)

**Power Reward**: -0.000002 * |torque * dof_vel|

**Bio CCF Reward** (Stage 2 only, w=0.15):
- Stance leg (contact>10N) → reward CCF>0 (stiff)
- Swing leg (no contact) → reward CCF<0 (compliant)
- 발목+무릎 모두 적용

**AMP Discriminator Reward**: task/disc 비율은 curriculum으로 조절
- epoch < 10000: task=0.7, disc=0.3
- epoch >= 10000: task=0.3, disc=0.7
